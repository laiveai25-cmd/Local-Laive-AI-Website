**FeatureItem** — icon + title + description, as a glass tile or a bare row. Use for "Why choose Laive AI" reasons, capability grids, and value props.

```jsx
<FeatureItem icon="target" index="01." title="Outcome-driven, not tool-driven"
  description="We start with your business goals, then design the right AI workflows to achieve them." />
<FeatureItem variant="row" icon="shield-check" title="Risk-managed deployment" />
```

Variants: `tile` (glass, default) and `row` (no background). Optional `index`.
