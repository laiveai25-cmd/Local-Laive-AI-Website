**StatBlock** — a headline metric with gold-gradient or 3D-copper numerals. Use for ROI figures, uptime, and proof points.

```jsx
<StatBlock value="60" suffix="%" label="ROI realized" sub="Typical 50–60% return within the first cycle." />
<StatBlock value="24/7" tone="copper" label="Always-on" align="center" />
```

Tones: `gold` (gradient, default), `copper` (3D extruded), `plain`. `align` left or center.
