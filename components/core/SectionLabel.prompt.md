**SectionLabel** — the gold Michroma eyebrow that sits above nearly every section heading in the Laive AI system.

```jsx
<SectionLabel>Our AI Products</SectionLabel>
<SectionLabel bullet={false} rule>How we work</SectionLabel>
```

Renders uppercase, wide-tracked display type with a hex bullet (toggle `bullet`) and optional trailing `rule`. Keep it short — 1-4 words.
