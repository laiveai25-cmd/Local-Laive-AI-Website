**Badge** — compact label, tag, or status pill.

```jsx
<Badge variant="gold">New</Badge>
<Badge variant="soft" icon={<Icon name="sparkles" size={12} />}>AI-native</Badge>
<Badge variant="success" dot>Live 24/7</Badge>
```

Variants: `gold`, `soft` (default), `outline`, `glass`, `neutral`, and status `success | warning | danger | info`. Sizes `sm | md | lg`. `dot` adds a glowing status dot.
